Common questions

How do I type a measurement like 12′ 6 3/8″?
Tap 1, 2, then ft ′, then 6, then the 3/8 chip above the keys. The inch mark is optional.

How do I fix a number without starting over?
Tap the number in the line you typed. It gets an outline, and the keys now change that number.

What does Pro add?
The answers of the solvers (stairs, rafters, right triangle, concrete, drywall, paint, flooring, roofing, studs, board feet), projects you can save and share as a PDF, and a history without the 20-line limit. The calculator itself is free, and Pro is one payment with no subscription.

I bought Pro and it is locked on a new phone.
Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Are the stair and rafter answers code-compliant?
The app does the geometry and warns about the usual U.S. residential stair limits. It does not check structural capacity or your local code. Check those before you cut.
