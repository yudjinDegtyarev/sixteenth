Privacy Policy

Sixteenth for iPhone. Effective October 14, 2026.

The short version

The app does not collect, send or sell any information about you. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code that tracks you.

What stays on your phone

Your calculations, the history, saved projects and settings are stored only on your device. They leave it only in two cases:

  • when you share a result yourself, as text or as a PDF, to the place you choose;
  • when your phone is backed up by Apple, if you have backups turned on. That backup is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Deleting the app deletes this data from the device.

Purchases

The Pro unlock is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment. The developer never sees your name, your card or your Apple Account; the app only learns from Apple whether Pro is unlocked.

Support e-mail

If you write to the support address, your e-mail address and whatever you put in the message are used only to answer you.

Children

The app is a calculator and is not directed at children. Since it collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text will be published on this page with a new date.
